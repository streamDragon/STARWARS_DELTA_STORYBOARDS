## Required visual language
- A single continuous asteroid mining facility, with interlocking rock masses and industrial construction embedded into the rock.
- Staggered floors, diagonal transitions, short bridges, vertical structural anchors, enclosed mine galleries and deliberate open flight pockets.
- Rock geometry has varied, substantial silhouettes across the whole height. Avoid a repeated top/bottom sprite frame around empty space.
- Infrastructure has structural continuity: floors meet slopes, slopes meet landings, columns anchor into rock and equipment belongs to real platforms.
- Cool blue crystal/light accents versus warm amber machinery, with a strong orange reactor destination. Do not use a generic starfield as the main visual content.
- Multiple upper/lower routes connect through deliberate openings. Preserve four-player scale and maneuvering; the image is treated as an overall art/layout reference and must be decomposed into actual camera windows before build.
- One large reactor complex integrated into the facility, not an isolated ring floating over four repeated trusses.
- Select a coherent material/rendering style. Existing small flat catalog parts mixed with the new shaded reactor do not establish a matching look.
