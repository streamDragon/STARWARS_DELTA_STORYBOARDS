## Semantic film grammar
Threat -> Human reaction -> Order -> Visible military action -> Consequence -> Changed decision.
ESTABLISH_SCALE, REVEAL_THREAT, HUMAN_REACTION, TACTICAL_ORDER, WEAPON_CAUSE_EFFECT, BREAK_FORMATION, INTERNAL_CHAOS, SACRIFICE_DECISION are internal *planning labels*, NOT new JSON enum values, MOVES or components.
Each beat has one central readable dramatic claim and one consequence that is observable in the next beat. A note in JSON does not make an effect happen.
Reference visual language: capital ship dwarfing escorts; slow reveal and calm holds; commands with target geography; close-up mechanical charge followed by external fire; a causal hit that persists; return to person in danger.
Do not imitate game footage frame-for-frame or copy dialogue/music/art.

## New 90 second film: THE LAST ESCORT
0-6: Large fleet traverses frame in three contiguous CROSSING_FLYBY phases; large ship anchors horizon, small escorts establish scale.
6-10: Detail on weapon/engine. Camera must track moving and rotating ship in mid-clip, not at completed progress=1.
10-15: Captain gives formation and targeting order in separate dialogue window.
15-18: Worried response, silent and clearly timed after order.
18-24: Lead escort crosses into a vulnerable lane, readable left-to-right orientation.
24-30: The first defense fails and one escort visibly falls behind or is disabled. The failure changes tactics.
30-34: Human acknowledges loss, separate dialogue interval.
34-37: Emotion reaction to specific loss, no overlapping MOVE.
37-42: Captain changes decision: use the environment to open a firing lane.
42-48: Escorts reposition near dangerous cover, preserve direction and anchor.
48-53: Energy builds on local cannon anchor; VFX, sound and mechanical movement must be checked separately.
53-58: Three staggered bolts visibly exit the correct muzzle, target remains in frame.
58-64: Impacts land on the intended target, not world origin; shield/nodes fail and visible damage persists.
64-69: Report confirms breakthrough with cost; dialogue interval.
69-72: Emotional response to casualty, not celebratory reset.
72-80: Retreat formation and rescue movement show consequence.
80-84: Last order makes the human decision explicit.
84-90: Wide aftermath shows scarred ship, smoke/fire/debris or explicit placeholder warning, surviving escorts move away.
