============================================================
CAMERA FIRST - HARD RULE
============================================================

STARWARS_DELTA is 2D.
Choose camera framing first, then normalized screen position and desired screen occupancy.

CAMERA DEFINES THE FRAME.
SCREEN FRACTION DEFINES VISUAL SIZE.
REAL RENDERER BOUNDS DEFINE UNITY SCALE.

Do not translate large/small/near/far into arbitrary raw Unity scale.
Typical framing references may use roughly 0.12 frame width for wide, 0.24 for medium and 0.42 for close when appropriate to the asset and shot.
Unity recomputes actual scale from the active cutscene camera and the resolved asset bounds.

============================================================
DIALOGUE NEAR / FAR - HARD RULE
============================================================

For normal locked two-person portrait dialogue:
ACTIVE SPEAKER = NEAR / larger / dominant.
NON-SPEAKING LISTENER = FAR / smaller / still visible.

When the speaker changes, the emphasis reverses without changing either character identity.
This is a system-owned Dialogue Stage presentation rule. DEVORA authors speaker/listener/mood and cinematic intent, not raw portrait scale.

Locked portrait dialogue uses Hold or supported cut-based coverage. Do not add decorative continuous Push/Pull/Track/Follow merely to keep the frame moving.

Dialogue must not automatically erase important world evidence. If the beat requires a blockade, battle, hazard, planet reveal, convoy or other essential world subject to remain visible, author that evidence in the beat. The Adapter/V3 Director must choose a legal world/monitor/overlay presentation rather than blindly switching to portrait-only FACE_TO_FACE.

============================================================
STORY EVIDENCE - HARD RULE
============================================================

Physical story claims require serialized visible proof:
STORY CLAIM -> VISIBLE ELEMENTS -> ACTION / CHANGE -> CONSEQUENCE -> FINAL STATE.

Examples:
- fleet arrives: the fleet must enter/reveal and remain visibly present.
- ship destroyed: target visible -> attack/impact -> destroyed/absent final state.
- convoy escapes: visible convoy -> movement/exit -> visible escape consequence.

Dialogue describing an unseen physical event is not visual evidence.
If essential evidence cannot be represented honestly with CURRENT assets/routes, report one explicit gap rather than inventing a substitute.

============================================================
IDENTITY + ROUTE SAFETY
============================================================

Narrative identity is not visual similarity.
Distinct named people stay distinct unless sameIdentityAs explicitly opts into shared identity.
A portrait does not become a WorldActor merely because it depicts the character.
An Effect does not become an Actor because it moves.
A Layer depicting a ship does not become an Actor.
Animation compatibility is exact and Unity-owned.

DEVORA should choose semantic identities and legal visible evidence from CURRENT. The Simple Adapter resolves the exact backend route.
