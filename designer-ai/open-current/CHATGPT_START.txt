AUTONOMOUS FIRST-PASS FILM CONTRACT
- Produce ONE complete CUTSCENE_SCRIPT_V1 candidate from the fresh verified CURRENT without waiting for Unity. Candidate creation is autonomous. When an authorized local Unity lint path is available, final downloadable-file delivery requires the canonical STRICT file Candidate Lint to PASS; Studio Preview recovery is only an editing/review convenience and must not be used to hide schema, CURRENT, camera-subject, action-subject or target errors.
- ONE WORLD ACTOR, ONE NAME: for a ship/person participating in actions or camera movement, prefer the same stable cast[].id and visible[].id in every beat. actions[].subject and camera.subject use that cast[].id. Do not create a second visible alias such as cast.id=interceptor with visible.id=hero_ship for the same ship; this can materialize duplicates or break camera binding. Only intentionally separate instances get separate ids and staging.
- TARGET-DEPENDENT CAMERA: push, pull, zoom, zoom_in, zoom_out, zoom_in_out, follow and track all lower to camera behavior that requires a concrete focal WorldActor. Author camera.subject explicitly for each such beat. The named cast.id must resolve to exactly one visible physical Actor in the same beat. Never target an implied, unspawned or ambiguous clone. If there is no unique physical target, choose a legal non-target-dependent camera movement instead.
- TARGETED ACTIONS: fire/attack/react/impact/destroy must reference subjects and targets that resolve to uniquely visible physical Actors in that beat when the action semantics require visibility. Never rely on Preview to guess a single remaining Actor, drop a target, skip an action, or retarget an alias. If the intended target is not visible, stage it visibly or author a different legal action.
- BUILD ONE COHERENT FRAME: choose one actual CURRENT Layer as the main shot background and treat it as full-frame; only add secondary scenery whose real preview shows a compositable layer/transparent prop. An opaque rectangular landscape/structure is not a foreground sticker over unrelated space art. If a full environmental image is essential, give it its own establishing shot or choose a truly compatible layered asset.
- SCREEN SAFE AREA: unless an intentional edge reveal or departure, keep the principal subject's complete projected bounds inside the active camera: centerX approximately 0.12..0.88, centerY 0.15..0.85, and verify any authored screenWidthFraction fits the remaining margins. In normal wide fleet shots, start ships around 0.12..0.25 frame width, separate their lanes and make the threat's scale visibly different; purposeful close-ups may be larger, but accidental cropping and stacked ships are not drama.
- PHYSICAL CAUSE AND REACTION: each launch/hit has a readable source, direction, target and later visible response. Use explicit schema-legal actions/intervals, frame composition and original animationHandle. The text 'the ship was hit' is not a substitute for a visual response.
- STORY GEOGRAPHY: begin with who is where and the travel direction, change the audience's information or emotion with each cut, and end with an observable consequence. Favor a readable establishing/approach/target/reaction/aftermath sequence over repeated unrelated flybys and effects.
- SELF-REVIEW BEFORE EXPORT (not Lint): trace every world actor from cast to visible to camera/action target; inspect real asset pixels; mentally frame each beat using its normalized coordinates and widths; confirm backgrounds fit, characters are visible, distinct ships are spaced, actions have consequences, and every beat changes something. If any fails, refine the authored V1 BEFORE delivering it. Do not change runtime/backend fields.

CAMERA
Camera authoring is semantic directing intent.
- framing/movement/subject/direction/intensity/parallax use only matching schema values.
- camera.intensity is a closed enum with exactly subtle, medium, strong. Do not use low/high here; those words belong to other fields in the schema and are not interchangeable.
- camera.subject is semantic composition intent and is not automatically a physical Transform target.
- Hold may preserve semantic composition without a physical target.
- Follow/Track may bind an active legal WorldActor when runtime support exists.
- Pan supports left/right/up/down reveal; up/down is the current 2D tilt-style reveal route.
- camera.parallax moves legal physical scenery layers with depth-scaled offsets and never moves UI/Overlay; locked dialogue remains stable.
- Push/Pull mean continuous within-shot framing change, not merely a cut to another static lens size.
- Drift/Pan/Shake/ImpactShake must be visibly meaningful when Unity executes them; technical non-zero movement that is imperceptible is not exact success.
- Camera Orbit is not a legal Simple V1 camera movement in this CURRENT. Use Actor motionIntent=orbit for fixed-center Actor Orbit; never invent an unseen 3D side/back view.
- LOCKED DIALOGUE CAMERA IS A BLOCKING AUTHORING RULE: any dialogue beat that resolves to locked portrait, monitor, or radio coverage may use only camera.movement=hold or camera.movement=cut (cut-based Snap). push/pull/follow/track/pan/drift/shake/impact_shake are forbidden in that beat. Put cinematic camera movement in an adjacent non-dialogue beat. Validate this before V3 lowering; do not rely on Unity to discover it later.

BACKGROUND MOTION
- Place optional backgroundMotion on the beat that owns an exact CURRENT locationHandle, outside camera/visible/actions: "backgroundMotion":{"direction":"left","speed":0.6,"loop":true}.
- direction means the background image travels on screen and allows only left, right, up, down, up_left, up_right, down_left, down_right. speed is 0..5 cinematic world units per second (default 0.6); loop is boolean (default true).
- Keep actor paths and animation independent. This is environmental movement and can use a hold camera. Repeat the field in each beat where motion should continue; inspect actual coverage/seams because repeat/wrap depends on renderer support. Missing locationHandle or locked dialogue makes Studio ignore backgroundMotion with a warning; put travel beside locked dialogue.

DIALOGUE
Dialogue is closed-world.
- speaker/listener must reference an existing cast[].id exactly.
- Resolve that local cast id first, then validate the referenced cast[].identityHandle against EMOTIONAL_DIALOGUE_CURRENT.
- cast[].id does not need to equal the published actorId or identityHandle; it is only a local movie reference.
- Unknown or misspelled speaker/listener aliases are errors. Never fuzzy-match or silently reinterpret them as direct identities.
- dialogue cast identityHandle must equal a published authoringReady dialogue character identityHandle.
- expressionIntent is opt-in, not a per-line default. Omit it for ordinary dialogue and let the exact CharacterPack published defaultExpression apply. Add expressionIntent only for a deliberate visible acting beat; then resolve speaker -> cast[].identityHandle -> the exact authoringReady dialogue character and require the token to appear literally in that character supportedExpressions. Never auto-generate Determined/Concerned/Urgent/Relieved/Shocked/Neutral from dialogue wording, mood or delivery. If the desired expression is absent, omit expressionIntent.
- do not invent identity, expression or presentation values from generic Catalog evidence.

AUDIO
- each authored cue uses kind + exact CURRENT route=Audio handle.
- repeated uses remain separate authored occurrences.
- visual Effects and projectiles do not automatically supply sound.
- generated simple_audio_* identities are backend-only.

SILENT AUDIO POLICY
- Audio is optional in CUTSCENE_SCRIPT_V1. A silent cutscene is legal.
- When CURRENT exposes zero route=Audio handles, omit beat.audio entirely. Never invent or infer an audio handle.
- Missing/unavailable CURRENT audio is a non-blocking YELLOW warning: AUDIO_UNAVAILABLE_IN_CURRENT. It must not block schema validation, Editable Preview or Build.
- If an explicit audio cue is authored, its handle must still be an exact CURRENT route=Audio handle; an explicit invalid/fabricated cue remains an authoring error.
- For a requested full cinematic scene, when CURRENT exposes suitable Layer/Effect/Audio handles, deliberately establish location, atmosphere and timed accents instead of leaving the scene visually empty. This is authoring guidance, not a blocker: intentionally minimal or silent films remain legal.

CUTSCENE PROJECTILES / MISSILES
- every type=fire action requires a schema-legal projectileId.
- projectileId is closed-world and comes from the matching schema.
- count is a real authored quantity.
- projectile identity is never an Effect handle, filename, gameplay prefab name or fuzzy match.
- target/anchor intent must remain exact when authored.
- launcher, muzzle transform, collider wiring, travel mechanics and Timeline receiver implementation remain Unity-owned.

CONCURRENCY
The system must support legal simultaneous operations rather than serializing them merely to avoid conflicts. Examples include:
- actor animation + actor movement;
- actor animation + camera movement;
- moving shooter + fire;
- two-way fire + camera shake;
- camera framing change + a separate compatible perspective property.
One runtime property/capability still has one effective owner for the same interval.

ACCEPTED FIXTURE / BACKEND REPAIR DISCIPLINE
Once a legal CUTSCENE_SCRIPT_V1 fixture passes authoring integrity, downstream BACKEND/ENGINE failures are repaired against that same authored fixture.
- do not rewrite legal beats/timing/handles/camera intent/animation intent/projectile counts/targets merely to hide runtime failure;
- fixture names, beat IDs, actor IDs and timestamps never become production special cases;
- compile-only success is not movie-quality proof;
- runtime PASS requires actual Unity execution of the final saved/reopened Editable Preview.

FAIL-SOFT DISCIPLINE
- Author strict, Studio tolerant.
- deterministic safe repair may continue with YELLOW/ORANGE diagnostics.
- never report fallback/degradation as exact success.
- preserve the last valid Editable Preview when a new candidate fails where possible.
- legal source intent lost after lowering/materialization is BACKEND/ENGINE-owned, not a reason to corrupt source authoring.

FIRST-PASS PRESENTATION-TIMELINE CHECK (MANDATORY BEFORE DELIVERING NEW V1):
Compute absolute beat boundaries from 0 by summing beats[].durationSeconds. For EVERY root animeEmotion[] interval [startSeconds, startSeconds + durationSeconds), compare it to EVERY beat referenced by moves[].beatIds and EVERY beat that has dialogue. If max(start1,start2) < min(end1,end2), there is forbidden presentation overlap. Keep anime inserts in dedicated, non-dialogue, non-MOVE beats. Check overlap with MOVES as well as dialogue.
