STARWARS_DELTA ART SELECTION POLICY (2026-10-26)

Select art by the requested cinematic role, verified visual appearance, and legally published CURRENT capabilities. There is no blanket MY_Art-first rule. Assets/_Game/MY_Core/MY_Art, Assets/Playniax, and other project folders compete on the same evidence-based footing.

For PRIMARY ANIMATED SPACECRAFT, inspect legally published Playniax Space Shooter Art Pack 01 and 02 candidates, plus other CURRENT animation-capable packs, BEFORE choosing an example Nautolan ship. Nautolan is an eligible option, not the default. Do not automatically use a frequently featured or alphabetically early item.

Search the full categoryChunks and matching AUTHORING_HANDLES.json, not just the 32 featured Actors. Check sourcePackCoverage and ANIMATION_RETRIEVAL_INDEX.json. A route=Actor PNG or individual frame (for example launcher0000) is a static world visual, NOT proof of playable sprite animation. Only a legal route=Animation animationHandle with verified directMovieMaterial is a playable direct Sprite animation cue.

If a desired installed Unity pack has no matching legal published CURRENT handle, state CURRENT_COVERAGE_GAP. Never invent a handle, claim that a static frame is an AnimationClip, or silently substitute Nautolan. A catalog rescan and existing Smart Publish pipeline are required before newly eligible materials may be used.

Inspect real preview pixels for important choices; names and descriptions alone are insufficient. Keep the original V1 source, and report unresolved handles and visible placeholder count separately from an editable import PASS. For acceptance probes do not modify the submitted script to make it pass.

Never modify DGraphics to satisfy catalog selection. Published CURRENT is canonical for authoring; Unity/Plastic is canonical for implementation. The index is shared on the Storyboards site, not in a private Work directory.
