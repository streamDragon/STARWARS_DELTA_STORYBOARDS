AUTHOR THE FILM · DO NOT REDESIGN THE SYSTEM
NORMAL CUTSCENE AUTHORING IS NOT AN ARCHITECTURE TASK. Do not create, propose, require or simulate a new schema, IR, Director Plan, compiler, resolver, camera system, timing system, audio system, pipeline stage or backend contract in order to author a film. The existing STARWARS_DELTA path is the system. Adapt cinematic intent into what the verified CURRENT + live Simple V1 + existing V3/V5 backend can already represent.
When a cinematic idea is supported
Use the existing legal vocabulary and CURRENT assets directly.
When a cinematic idea is not directly supported
Preserve its story purpose using legal 2D composition, cuts, existing camera moves, legal actions and beat/state changes. If an essential fact still cannot be represented honestly, report one explicit gap. Do not solve the gap by inventing system architecture.
External film/video systems are inspiration, not requirements. A reference to Seedance, film grammar, editing language or another tool may inspire pacing and staging, but it never authorizes new STARWARS_DELTA fields, subsystems or pipeline layers.
Engineering discussion is not authoring capability. Planned projections, code-review comments, future installers, experimental runtime ideas or implementation notes do not become usable film capabilities until the matching verified CURRENT / live schema exposes them. Do not author against a capability merely because someone discussed or implemented it elsewhere.
READY TO AUTHOR
Do not ask the designer to manually provide a Catalog, schema, handles list or a separate “V3B validation report”.
When this guide is reached through DEVORA, use the sealed verified Open CURRENT, the matching Visual Atlas, the exact Simple V1 schema and the matching CURRENT semantic handles. If a development change is not present in verified Open CURRENT, treat it as unavailable and report one explicit gap. A milestone name such as V3B does not make an unpublished asset authorable.
Do not ask the designer to direct the cutscene for you.
The designer may provide only a few lines of story. Infer ordinary cinematic decisions yourself: sequence, beats, shot purpose, framing, staging, screen direction, pacing, reveal structure and payoff. Ask only when an ambiguity would materially change identity, story meaning, a must-happen event or another non-inferable requirement.

2D LEGALITY

USE
X/Y screen movement and clear left/right/up/down direction
Foreground / midground / background as 2D compositing layers
Relative scale, overlap and supported parallax
Cuts, reaction cuts, Wide / Medium / Close / Detail
Hold / Push / Pull / Follow / Track / Cut when they operate on the 2D composition
NEVER ASSUME
True 3D Z geometry or camera orbit around a flat sprite
Perspective/Y rotation or a view angle absent from CURRENT pixels
Moving behind a flat sprite as if it had volume
Fake 3D staging to compensate for missing assets
Raw Unity scale guessed from huge/tiny/near/far
Depth without fake 3D: use parallax, layer order, relative scale, overlap, screen-space arcs and cuts. If a shot only works after inventing a new view angle, redesign the shot inside the existing system, not the system around the shot.

Story Claim→Camera Framing→FG/MG/BG→Screen X/Y→Screen Fraction→Motion→Payoff
GOOD AUTHORING

UNITY RESOLUTION
visibleHeight = orthographicSize * 2
visibleWidth  = visibleHeight * cameraAspect
desiredWorldWidth = visibleWidth * screenWidthFraction
scale = desiredWorldWidth / naturalSpriteWidth

MEDIUM / CLOSE

Wide is normal. Close is earned. Do not make every panel a portrait. If the viewer cannot tell where the character is, what surrounds them or how large the threat is, the storyboard has become passport photos with explosions.
READABILITY PRIORITY: LOCATION → FOCAL SUBJECT → RELATIONSHIP → DIRECTION → UI/DIALOGUE → ACCENTS.

CINEMATIC MOTION

PRIMARY 60–70%
story action / focal subject
SUPPORT 20–30%
camera, escort, reaction, secondary threat
AMBIENT ~10%
stars, smoke, traffic, haze
A MOVE IS SETUP → VISUAL TRANSITION → PAYOFF. THE NAME OF A MOVE IS NOT ENOUGH.
CINEMATIC MOVE NAME ≠ JSON ACTION TYPE. Flyby, Approach, Intercept, Pursuit, Pass Camera and Formation Break are directing vocabulary. Translate them into beats + legal actions + visible state changes + legal camera intent. Never write "type":"flyby", "type":"approach" or another cinematic move name unless the live schema explicitly contains that action type.
Flyby
enter off-screen → cross readable vector → optional track → exit
Approach
small/distant → grows → reaches screen mark
Intercept
two vectors converge → readable attack/avoidance payoff
Pullback reveal
hero shrinks while more environment/threat appears
Pass camera
large near edge → crosses/crops → exits
Formation break
formation established → members diverge in X/Y → new readable state
Continuity: preserve meaningful screen direction. A reversal must be shown. Camera subject is optional; no target is better than a false target. Travel requires move / enter / exit. Animation alone does not prove displacement.
TIMING RHYTHM · GUIDES, NOT SCHEMA LIMITS
Establishing
~3–7 sec
Long enough to read location and scale.
Reveal
~2–5 sec
Setup, appearance, readable payoff.
Reaction
~1.5–4 sec
Cause must already be readable or remain visible.
Dialogue coverage
usually ~3–7 sec
Driven by line length, performance and world evidence.
Action beat
~1–4 sec
Faster when vector/cause is already established.
Impact / aftermath hold
~0.8–2.5 sec after event
Do not cut before consequence is perceptible.
Large reveal / aftermath
~3–6 sec
Give scale and consequence time to land.
TIMING SERVES READABILITY AND DRAMA. THESE RANGES ARE AUTHORING GUIDES, NOT SCHEMA RULES. A shot ends when its story evidence has become readable, not when a stopwatch reaches a sacred number.
Internal directing may be richer than serialization. You may reason about pauses, reaction timing, sound emphasis, music pressure, audio lead, hard cuts, match-like transitions or performance beats when they help direct the scene. But if the live Simple V1 schema has no legal field for that concept, do not invent one. Express what can be expressed through legal timing, mood, visible states, actions and camera; leave system-owned realization to the existing backend.
Explicit intent beats inferred defaults. Inferred pacing, performance, transition or camera choices may fill gaps, but they must never override explicit story facts or explicit legal semantic intent already supplied by the designer/script. Do not mechanically map every Impact to one transition or every finale to Crossfade; choose only among capabilities actually exposed by CURRENT/schema and according to the shot's dramatic purpose.

1 · EXACT

2 · LEGAL EQUIVALENT
Preserve the story purpose with existing shots, cuts, camera moves, actions and state changes.

Drop or simplify non-essential decoration that the existing system cannot honestly express.

LEGAL EQUIVALENT MAY CHANGE PRESENTATION, NEVER THE ESSENTIAL STORY FACT. It must preserve identity, essential quantity, essential location, cause/effect, who acts on whom, who is harmed/saved/destroyed/wins, must-happen events, and the visible evidence required by the storyClaim. If preserving those invariants is impossible, stop at EXPLICIT GAP instead of rewriting the story.
NEVER USE A FIFTH STEP CALLED “BUILD A NEW SYSTEM”. Normal authoring stops at the best truthful film the existing system can produce.
ARRIVAL
fleet visible + enter/reveal + wide/pull → fleet remains visibly established
DESTRUCTION
source + fire + target + impact + destroy → destroyed/absent consequence
EVACUATION
convoy visible + threat + move/exit → visible safety consequence
Count is a visual obligation. If count=6, expand to six stable semantic instances, use one exact verified grouped CURRENT visual that genuinely represents six, or report a quantity/capability gap. Never silently reduce six to one.
Crowd / fleet variation: repeated entities must read as a group, not clones stamped from one template. Vary legal screen position, depth, screen fraction, entry timing and supported state/pose while preserving exact CURRENT identity and route. Avoid perfectly even spacing, identical Y, identical scale and synchronized entrances unless the story intentionally calls for mechanical formation. Never invent alternate assets merely to create variation.

IDENTITY, DIALOGUE & SEMANTICS

identityHandle = who/what this entity actually is.
presentationHandle = optional preferred same-identity presentation.
sameIdentityAs = explicit opt-in to shared identity.
Stable identity
GREEN PILOT stays GREEN PILOT. A same-identity portrait/body presentation may change. Never substitute RED PILOT or another similar-looking asset just to make resolution easier.
DISTINCT NAMED PEOPLE STAY DISTINCT UNLESS sameIdentityAs EXPLICITLY SAYS OTHERWISE.

When referring to a cast character, dialogue speaker/listener and camera.subject point to the local cast ID, not the long CURRENT handle.
DIALOGUE IS STILL A FILM SHOT
Legal dialogue JSON fields
speaker · listener · text · mood

Performance is directing intent resolved from legal semantics and context. Do not invent performance, emotionStrength, portrait scale, Dialogue Stage slots or V5 dialogue fields.
Near / Far rule
Active speaker = NEAR / larger / dominant.
Listener = FAR / smaller / still visible.
Speaker changes reverse emphasis without changing identity.
Mood ≠ backend expression field. Author rich mood semantically inside the legal mood string: concerned, urgent, defiant, vulnerable, relieved, suspicious, terrified, warm, exhausted, etc. Do not collapse mood into a remembered V5 emotion enum, and do not invent speakerExpression, emotion, speakerDistance, presentationPreset or similar fields. Unity maps semantic mood/context into supported expression and backend delivery.
DIALOGUE MUST NOT ERASE THE THING THE DIALOGUE IS ABOUT.
If dialogue concerns a blockade, hazard, convoy, battle or planet reveal, preserve that evidence through a world shot, monitor, overlay or other supported composition. Radar/tactical UI remains support unless the beat is explicitly about that information. Background art must not bake dialogue text, speaker names or portrait UI into the world.
Location change is state change. When locationHandle changes, non-continuing actors/layers/effects must end or hide before the next physical space is established.

FINAL PREFLIGHT

STORY CLAIM → VISIBLE ELEMENTS → ACTION / CHANGE → CONSEQUENCE → FINAL STATE
SCHEMA VALID IS NOT STORY TRUE. STORY TRUE REQUIRES VISIBLE EVIDENCE. A technically legal JSON that fails to show the claimed event, quantity, relationship or consequence is still a failed film.
REAL QA LESSON
Legal does not automatically mean coherent.
Inspect CURRENT pixels for view angle, style, scale relationship, location fit and intended role.
Warnings are graded.
Optional decoration/effect degradation may be YELLOW. Wrong identity, missing essential location/evidence, impossible quantity or principal visual failure is RED.
