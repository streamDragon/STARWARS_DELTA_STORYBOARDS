2D LEGALITY

USE
screen X/Y movement and explicit travel direction
foreground / midground / background compositing
scale change to imply approach or retreat
cuts, reaction cuts, overlap, parallax and negative space
Hold / Push / Pull / Follow / Track / Cut
DO NOT INVENT
true 3D orbit around a flat sprite
new perspective/Y rotation absent from CURRENT pixels
moving behind a flat sprite as if it has volume
raw Unity scale guessed from “huge” or “tiny”
fake 3D used to hide missing visual evidence
Depth without fake 3D: relative size, overlap, parallax, layer order, screen-space arcs, reveal timing and cuts are enough for most STARWARS_DELTA cinematics.

IDENTITY, DIALOGUE & SEMANTICS

identityHandle = who/what this entity is.
presentationHandle = optional same-identity visual presentation.
sameIdentityAs = explicit identity-sharing opt-in.
Never substitute identity for convenience.
If Green Pilot fails to resolve, do not show Red Pilot because “it looks close enough”. Report the gap.
DISTINCT NAMED PEOPLE STAY DISTINCT.
Near / Far dialogue
Speaker becomes larger/near; listener remains visible smaller/far. On speaker change, reverse emphasis without changing identity.
Locked dialogue camera
Prefer Hold or a cut-based reverse. Decorative continuous drift during locked portrait dialogue weakens readability and is not evidence of performance.

EDGE-CASE MATRIX

UNRESOLVED HANDLE
Expected: blocker diagnostic, no substitute asset.
Fixture: EXAMPLE_DIAGNOSTIC_UNRESOLVED.json
COUNT > 1
Expected: stable repeated instances or a genuinely grouped CURRENT visual.
SCREEN FRACTION > 1
Expected: intentional crop/overscan, not automatic clamping to “safe” framing.
PREVIEW USABLE ≠ PUBLISH READY
Expected: preview may proceed with a visible warning; do not call the asset publish-safe.
NEEDS REVIEW
Expected: keep review-limited status visible. Never silently upgrade confidence.
ANIMATION FAMILY
Expected: one semantic/canonical creative choice such as ROBOT WALK; technical frames remain provenance/runtime details.
IDENTITY MISMATCH
Expected: block instead of replacing a named actor with a similar-looking one.
NO CAMERA SUBJECT
Expected: allowed when composition or atmosphere is the subject; false subject is worse than omitted subject.
