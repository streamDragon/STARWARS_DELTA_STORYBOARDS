# Visual-selection lesson: legal assets can still compose badly

## Real observation
A Preview built successfully but a large robot visually overwhelmed a Doctor, and a corridor panel looked like a small picture floating inside later space shots.

## GOOD
After technical validity, preserve readable scale, grounding and location coherence. Prefer reviewed/preferred representatives and environment assets whose composition actually fills the intended cinematic role.

## Lesson
Catalog legality answers "may I use it?". Visual QA still answers "does this look like the requested movie?".
