# CASE 021 - Legal space asset is not necessarily a useful black-hole shot

## Observed
A giant red/orange Midground dominated a Black Hole Distress Preview. Actor size had improved, but the scene did not communicate black hole, gravity shear or rescue hierarchy.

## GOOD
Normalize Midground/Foreground independently from full-frame backgrounds, and select assets by dramatic semantic evidence. Readability priority: location, focal actor, relationship, direction, UI/dialogue, then accents.
